# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.19

- **Shorter Discord replies.** Hear “Muted,” “Unmuted,” or “Deafened.” The Island’s mic turns red and draws a slash when mute is confirmed. Unmuting reverses the animation.
- **Screen sharing.** Say “Share screen” for Screen 1 or “Share screen two” for Screen 2. C.O.D.Y. switches to **Entire Screen** in Discord’s picker and looks for the requested display. “Discord screenshare” opens the picker so you can choose a source.
- **Leave a call.** Say “Leave Discord,” “Disconnect from Discord,” or “Hang up on Discord.” C.O.D.Y. confirms when you’ve left the current call or voice channel.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates** in C.O.D.Y., or download the x64 installer for Intel and AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM and Snapdragon PCs.
