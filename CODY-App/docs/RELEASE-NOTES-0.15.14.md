# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.14

- **Liquid unfold.** The compact island opens from a tiny slit at the top edge. It widens from the center, dips down, and rebounds before the mascot and any active media or status details fade in.
- **Animation Studio.** Choose **Unfold / hide** under **Test all animations** to replay the reveal. The animation follows your speed and reduced-motion settings.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates** in C.O.D.Y., or download the x64 installer for Intel and AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM and Snapdragon PCs.
