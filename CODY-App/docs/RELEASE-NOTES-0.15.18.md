# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.18

- **Discord in the Island.** Typed and spoken commands show progress, then the result. Try “Discord mute,” “Discord defen,” or “share screen two on Discord.”
- **Background controls.** Discord commands leave your active window in focus. A minimized Discord window briefly restores behind it and minimizes again. A completely covered Discord window can still fail to confirm a change.
- **Share picker.** Say “Discord screenshare” to open the picker and choose a source. Sharing requires a voice channel or call in the Discord desktop app.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates** in C.O.D.Y., or download the x64 installer for Intel and AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM and Snapdragon PCs.
