# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.20

- **Stop sharing in Discord.** Say “Stop screen share” or “Discord stop streaming.” The Island shows **Share stopped** once Discord confirms it, and you stay in the call.
- **Share in the connected call.** C.O.D.Y. opens the call you’re connected to before using its share controls, even if Discord is showing another channel.
- **Dismiss the Island.** Clicking the transparent area around an expanded panel now collapses it.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates** in C.O.D.Y., or download the x64 installer for Intel and AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM and Snapdragon PCs.
