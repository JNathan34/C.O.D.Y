# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.36

- **Plex and Overseerr in chat.** Ask who is watching Plex or check whether a movie or show is available. Results include posters, **Watch on Plex** and **View on Overseerr** buttons, and request controls for missing titles or seasons.
- **Plex setup and startup.** C.O.D.Y. reopens Plugins after sign-in so you can pick your server. It also retries a dropped Plex connection once during a lookup.
- **Faster push-to-talk feedback.** Press Ctrl+Alt to see the listening waveform as the microphone opens, even while a task update or spoken reply is showing. You can also type your next message during speech playback.
- **Island task expressions.** Request stopped uses bouncing square eyes, Request failed uses exclamation marks, and approval and input requests use question marks. Drafting notes gets animated pencil eyes.
- **Screen drawings and layout.** Drawings stay with their original window and view when you switch apps. Plugin cards line up, and the chat box fits inside its panel.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates**, or download the x64 installer for Intel/AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM/Snapdragon PCs.
