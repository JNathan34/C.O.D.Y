# C.O.D.Y. 0.15.28

- **Dismiss an individual highlight.** Click inside or near a ring around a recognised button or quiz choice. Your click reaches the app, and C.O.D.Y. clears only that ring. Other highlights and freehand drawings stay in place.
- **Keep highlights with their targets.** Screen markings track their targets independently as you scroll, including on displays with Windows scaling enabled. They hide when you switch away or the highlighted content changes and can return when the original content is visible again. [Issue #37](https://github.com/JNathan34/C.O.D.Y/issues/37)
- **See speed-test results without losing pinned stats.** The Island shows download and upload progress, then displays the measured speeds for five seconds. Your pinned cards, such as RAM usage, return automatically.

## Install or update

Use **Settings → Updates**, or download the x64 installer for Intel and AMD PCs or the ARM64 installer for ARM and Snapdragon PCs.
